Customer

Synthetic data seeding

Generates a realistic customer base with no real PII, for development and demos.

Service customer-service :8082Endpoint POST /api/v1/admin/seed-dataVolume 5,000 customers / ~8,000 accounts

Step by step

  1. AdminController.seedDatabase() delegates to the seeder and wraps the result in an ApiResponse.
  2. DataSeederService.seedDatabase() returns early when customers already exist, so the endpoint is safe to call twice.
  3. Each customer gets 1–3 accounts (about 8,000 in total), linked through Customer.accounts with CascadeType.ALL.
  4. Every 1,000 customers, saveAll() writes the chunk; the converter encrypts every generated ID number.
