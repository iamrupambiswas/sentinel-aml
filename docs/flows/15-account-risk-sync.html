How customer-service publishes each account's effective risk rating so detection-service can apply the customer-risk multiplier to every score.

Step by step

  1. Effective rating = the HIGHER of the customer's and the account's rating (RiskRatings.effective).
  2. Opening an account (optionally with a chosen id such as ACC-RAPID-01) or changing a rating updates MySQL in one transaction.
  3. The Redis write is registered as an afterCommit synchronization: a rolled-back change never reaches detection.
  4. Changing a CUSTOMER's rating republishes every one of their accounts.
  5. AccountRiskSyncService.syncAll() pages through accounts (3-column projection, 500 per page, one pipelined Redis call per page). It runs at startup, after seeding and on demand.
  6. detection-service reads the key per scored account; a missing key (other banks' accounts) means multiplier 1.0.
