Customer onboarding & PII

How a new customer is validated, traced, encrypted at rest and returned with the national ID masked.

Step by step

  1. LoggingAspect.logAround() intercepts every @RestController method, puts an 8-character trace id in the MDC and logs the request.
  2. CustomerController.registerCustomer() receives the body; @Valid triggers the constraints on CustomerRegistrationRequest.
  3. A violation raises MethodArgumentNotValidException (or HttpMessageNotReadableException for an unknown enum), which GlobalExceptionHandler turns into a 400 envelope.
  4. CustomerProfileServiceImpl.onboardCustomer() builds the entity with a new UUID, riskRating = MEDIUM and onboardedAt = now.
  5. When Hibernate writes the row, PIIAttributeConverter.convertToDatabaseColumn() encrypts idNumber with AES-256-GCM and a random 12-byte IV.
  6. The controller maps the saved entity to CustomerResponse.from(), which masks the ID number, and returns 201 Created.
